Welcome to CCU Builder

Plan your CCU chains on a price ruler. Everything stays on your computer.

  1. Load CCU Game CSV: your CCUs export from CCU Game (the CCUs one).

    No CSV yet? Click Try example data to play with a made-up hangar.

    Prices come from your CCU Game export. If you set speculative prices in CCU Game, those are what the app uses.

  2. Load ships CSV: your ships export. Each ship becomes a gray starter ship piece.
  3. Click Clear all chains to remove the example chains, then + Add chain for each ship you're aiming for (the end ship).
  4. Drag a starter ship onto a row's $0 slot, then drag CCUs from Your CCUs (owned) (left, under Your ships) after it. The white box shows where a piece fits.
  5. Don't own the ship you want to start from? + Add starter ship adds a planned ship (its price counts as missing).
  6. Save to file now and then as a backup. The page saves in your browser too, but browser data can be cleared. Load from file brings it back.

New: Suggested warbonds ★ (left, below your CCUs) suggests teal warbond pieces for the chain you click, or warbond and standard CCUs for a yellow gap, and each one you place goes on the 🛒 Shopping list.

This pop-up comes back if your browser data is cleared or you use a different browser. Click ? Help in the header to see it any time.

🛒 Shopping list

Every teal ★ planned CCU on your chains, in two sections. Warbonds are paid in cash: the last-seen warbond sale price minus the ship it follows (past sales, not a promise they come back). Standard CCUs are paid with store credit: today's store price difference (estimated, CIG doesn't sell every pair).

Email is best for short lists: long ones can get cut off. Use Copy or Download .txt for those.
